# Thirst Trap

Thirst Trap brings together queer nightlife events in Lowell and people interested in contributing to them.

## Language

**Event**:
A scheduled gathering with its own date, venue, and program.
_Avoid_: Series, application

**Event series**:
A recurring event identity, such as Thirst Trap or Spill the Tea. Interest in a series is distinct from applying to one scheduled event.

**Featured event**:
The upcoming event given the largest presentation on the homepage. It may be selected for prominence even when another event happens sooner.
_Avoid_: Up next, unless it is also the next chronological event

**Upcoming event**:
An announced event that has not ended, including an event currently in progress.

**Past event**:
An event that has ended. It is absent from public event listings, although its original event page remains accessible through a direct link.

**Application of interest**:
A person's expression of interest in contributing to future events in one or more roles. It is not a booking, accepted placement, or commitment to a shift.
_Avoid_: Booking, registration, confirmed signup

**Applicant**:
A person or business expressing interest in contributing to events.
_Avoid_: Attendee, unless referring to someone attending an event

**Role**:
A way an applicant wishes to contribute: DJ, drag performer, vendor, or volunteer. An application may include multiple roles.

**Volunteer assignment preference**:
The types of volunteer work an applicant is interested in, such as monitoring, door, coat check, or setup and breakdown. A preference is not an assigned shift.
_Avoid_: Role, when referring specifically to tasks within volunteering
